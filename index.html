INSTRUCTIONS

Controls

W/↑ Up   S/↓ Down   A/← Left   D/→ Right   M Switch World (Mirror map)   P/ESC Pause

Objective

Explore the map, collect keys and clues, avoid hazards, unlock restricted areas and reach the treasure before time runs out.

Collectibles

Coins (+10), Clues (+50), Keys (+100, unlock doors), Checkpoints (+75, saves your respawn point).

Hints

Spend score on four hint tiers: Basic Direction (-50), Navigation (-100), Advanced Path (-150), Key/Clue Location (-100). Score never goes below zero and hints have a short cooldown.

Computer Opponent

In Compete mode a real A* pathfinding opponent races you to the treasure. It respects walls, doors and objectives — it never teleports or cheats.

Lives & Health

Hazard tiles cost 25 health. At 0 health you lose a life and respawn at your last checkpoint. Lose all 3 lives and it's game over.
